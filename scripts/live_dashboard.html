What this is. The models are the ridge regressions from steps 6–7 of the project, trained offline on a historical BTCUSDT day and applied here unchanged. The Hawkes intensity is the one-step-ahead forecast with the parameters fitted that day.

How to read it. A few minutes of scores is noise; offline, the gains were 1–3 percentage points over whole days. The CI is per model; the gain lines ignore serial correlation, so treat them as indicative.
