# Multi-day validation: Binance BTCUSDT

Generated by `scripts/step6_binance_validation.py` on 7 BTCUSDT days (2024-06-03 to 2024-06-09, UTC, 24 h each, 5,681,120 aggressor events). Same Hawkes-fit code and same walk-forward test as the AAPL analysis (`docs/alpha_signal.md`). The dates in `config/holdout.json` (2024-07-01 to 07-07) have not been downloaded or evaluated.

Not a like-for-like replication of AAPL: aggTrades has no order book, so the baseline is trade flow only (`order_flow_imbalance`, `lambda_*_ewm`, `rv_60`, `trade_imbalance`); the price is the last trade of each second. A Binance aggTrade merges the fills of one taker order at one price, so its events are closer to orders than AAPL's prints.

## 2. Within-day walk-forward, each day separately

Step 5's test (5 chronological folds, Hawkes refit per training window, one-step-ahead features) run on each day. Each cell is that day's verdict for the alternative model against the baseline: **R** robust (both paired tests p < 0.0125), **S** suggestive (either p < 0.05), **·** null; **+** the alternative was better, **−** the baseline was better. Hit rates here and below count only moves larger than 1.5 ticks: 88% of non-zero one-second last-trade moves are a single tick, i.e. the bid-ask bounce, and a sign hit on a bounce says which side traded last rather than where the price went.

## 3. Cross-day: train on day d−1, test on day d

Hawkes parameters and ridge coefficients are fitted on the whole previous day, then applied unchanged to the next. ## Conclusion

- **+Hawkes:** cross-day it beats the baseline on 13 of 18 day×horizon return tests and on 5 of 6 days for next-second flow. p across days: ret_1s 0.0901, ret_5s 0.0586, ret_10s 0.205, ofi_1s 0.348 (Bonferroni threshold 0.0125; six test days give the test little power). Hit-rate gain on real moves: +2.2 to +2.8 pp.
- **+event-time:** cross-day it beats the baseline on 15 of 18 day×horizon return tests and on 6 of 6 days for next-second flow. p across days: ret_1s 0.0299, ret_5s 0.016, ret_10s 0.0235, ofi_1s 0.000557 (Bonferroni threshold 0.0125; six test days give the test little power). Hit-rate gain on real moves: +1.1 to +1.8 pp.

These are gross forecasting gains of a linear model on one week of one crypto pair, before any trading cost. They say the Hawkes / event-time features carry information the EWMA baseline lacks on this instrument; they do not say a strategy built on them is profitable (see `output/step4b_fixed/` for what happens to an AAPL market maker once fills are replayed from real trades).

## Limitations

- One instrument and one week; AAPL (`docs/alpha_signal.md`) gives a different answer, and that difference is not explained by the weaker BTCUSDT baseline (AAPL with the same flow-only features is still null on returns).
- No order book here: no spread, imbalance or microprice features, and no trading cost.
- The hour-of-day baseline is fitted on the same day it describes.
- Ridge α = 1 is not tuned; models are linear.
